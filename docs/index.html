1 Small Reaction Network Example (Thesis eq. 4.76)

Consider the classic reversible Michaelis-Menten enzyme-substrate reaction mechanism from Chapter 4 of the thesis:

$$S_1 + S_2 \underset{c_2}{\overset{c_1}{\rightleftharpoons}} S_3 \underset{c_4}{\overset{c_3}{\rightleftharpoons}} S_2 + S_4$$

With stoichiometric reaction rate constants:

$$c_1 = 1000, \quad c_2 = 500, \quad c_3 = 5, \quad c_4 = 1$$

Subject to closed linear conservation laws (Thesis Section 4.4.2): $$C \cdot N = b \implies \begin{cases} S_1 + S_3 + S_4 = 3 \\ S_2 + S_3 = 3 \end{cases}$$ Initial condition at $t = 0$: $N_1 = [3, 3, 0, 0]^T$ ($S_1=3, S_2=3, S_3=0, S_4=0$).

3 Full Infinitesimal Generator Matrix $D$ (Algorithm 3)

The master equation describes the continuous evolution of probabilities: $\frac{d P(t)}{dt} = D P(t)$. $D$ is a $10 \times 10$ sparse matrix containing 34 non-zero entries (10 diagonal + 24 transitions). Every column sum is strictly zero: $\sum_{i=1}^{10} D_{ij} = 0$, guaranteeing conservation of total probability.

5 CME Dimensionality Reduction & Projection (Algorithm 4)

Algorithm 4 re-indexes the 10 states by selecting the $d=4$ primary pivots $\{9, 8, 2, 6\}$. It constructs the coordinate transformation $T$ using the left eigenbasis $T_{nd}$ (Thesis Theorems 1, 2, 3), collapsing the stiff system into a $4 \times 4$ reduced linear system: $$\frac{d X(t)}{dt} = Q X(t), \quad X(0) = T_{nd}^T P(0)$$

Preservation of Probability (Thesis Theorem 3 & Corollary 1): Because column sums of $Q$ are strictly 0 and $\sum_{j=1}^4 X_j(0) = 1$, the reduced state preserves total probability at all continuous times: $$\sum_{j=1}^d X_j(t) = 1 \implies \sum_{i=1}^{10} \tilde{P}_i(t) = 1 \quad \forall t \ge 0$$

6 Multi-Step Preconditioned FSP (Algorithms 9, 10, 11, 12)

For unbounded or open systems, Algorithms 9–12 dynamically expand the truncated state space while using preconditioning to choose optimal step sizes $\tau_k \le t_\varepsilon$:

  • Algorithm 10 (Precondition): Solves the augmented error ODE system $\begin{pmatrix} \dot{Z} \\ \dot{Z}_\varepsilon \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} Q & 0 \\ -\mathbf{1}^T Q & 0 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} Z \\ Z_\varepsilon \end{pmatrix}$ to determine the maximum time step $\tau$ where leakage $Z_\varepsilon(\tau) \le \varepsilon$.
  • Algorithm 11 (Approximation): Computes the right eigenbasis $M_{nd}$ and evaluates the back-projection $P_{k+1} = T^{-1} \hat{M}_{nd} Z_{k+1}$.
  • Algorithm 12 (Continuous Transient Evaluator): Seamlessly reconstructs $P(t)$ for any query time $t$ by evaluating the exponential in the corresponding local interval snapshot.
